02

Why your data stays private

Your workspace file is selected and handled by your browser. It is not sent to HalloweenConyCoding, so we do not receive a copy of your personal tasks or calendar.

  • No uploadThe page reads the file you choose through your browser’s local permission.
  • No accountThere is no sign-in or user database holding your workspace content.
  • No server copyWhen connected, edits go back to your own file; this public page is only the interface.
